How it works

• মাটি যেভাবে কাজ করে

Records are linked

Deed registration (সাব-রেজিস্ট্রি), mutation (ই-নামজারি) and khatian data sit in one chain, so a mismatch shows up at once.

Checks run on their own

Khas land (খাস জমি), pending cases, bank liens and area differences between RS and BS are flagged without manual searching.

Copies are sealed

A cryptographic SHA-256 hash of every registered deed lets a buyer, bank or court confirm a paper copy is genuine.

Prototype for the LandTech 2026 hackathon, track Digital Land Records, Verification and Digitization, problem P5 with deed hashing from P2. Uses 100% synthetic dummy data only.
