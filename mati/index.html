How it works

Records are linked

Deed registration, mutation and khatian data sit in one chain, so a mismatch shows up at once.

Checks run on their own

Khas land, pending cases, bank liens and area differences between RS and BS are flagged without manual searching.

Copies are sealed

A hash of every registered deed lets a buyer, bank or court confirm a paper copy is genuine.

Prototype for the LandTech 2026 hackathon, track Digital Land Records, Verification and Digitization, problem P5 with deed hashing from P2. It uses dummy data only.
